### How good is the ranking?

No public ledger labels its duplicates, so 3,000 realistic duplicates were injected (re-keyed voucher, shifted date, transposed digits, vendor-name typos) and the pipeline was scored on finding them, holding out 30% of vendors.

| | Ranking model | Rule baseline (same vendor + amount, closest date) |
|---|---:|---:|
| PR-AUC | **0.52** | 0.04 |
| Precision in top 100 | **92%** | 0% |
| Precision in top 500 | **62%** | 2% |

- Candidate generation catches **93%** of injected duplicates; misses are mostly vendor-name typos that entity resolution refuses to merge.
- Precision is a lower bound: a few 'negatives' are probably real duplicates.
